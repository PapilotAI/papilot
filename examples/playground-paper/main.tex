\section{Philosophy: the page comes first}
\textbf{Read what your reader sees.}
Meaning depends on symbols, line breaks, figures and their context.
Papilot puts the review on the rendered page, so you can judge the actual result
without mentally compiling a source diff.

\textbf{Delegate the edit. Keep the judgment.}
The author decides.
Your manuscript stays, of course, under your control.
At any time, you can inspect the LaTeX behind an edit.

\textbf{Keep your tools and ownership.}
Your project remains ordinary local files. Git records the history and supports collaboration.
Use the coding agent you already have, with its own login or API key.
Papilot is free and open source, for macOS, Linux and Windows.

\section{A note has a precise address}
\begin{minipage}[c]{0.50\linewidth}
Mark a word, a symbol, or a region.
A note keeps its page and bounding box; an agent can use that location to find the source.
Normalized coordinates let the same box follow the page as you zoom or resize.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{0.45\linewidth}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{assets/notes.png}\par
{\scriptsize\color{ink}Figure 2. Notes retain the selected text or region.}
\end{minipage}\par
\vspace{5pt}
For an origin at $(0,0)$, let $W,H$ be page width and height and $b=(x_0,y_0,x_1,y_1)$ a selected box.
Its normalized coordinates are
\begin{equation}
  \widehat b=\left(\frac{x_0}{W},\frac{y_0}{H},\frac{x_1}{W},\frac{y_1}{H}\right).
\end{equation}

\section{Render first. Review. Then apply.}
Let $S$ be the source, $S'$ an agent's proposal, and $\mathcal R$ the LaTeX renderer.
The review compares the resulting pages:
\begin{equation}
  P=\mathcal R(S),\qquad P'=\mathcal R(S'),\qquad
  \Delta_{\rm PDF}=\mathcal D(P,P').
\end{equation}
Here $\mathcal D$ denotes the visual comparison. Text, equations and layout remain visible together.
For proposed edits $E$, the author chooses an accepted subset:
\begin{equation}
  E_{\rm accepted}=\{e\in E\mid\operatorname{decision}(e)=\mathrm{accept}\},
  \qquad S_{\rm next}=\operatorname{Apply}(S,E_{\rm accepted}).
\end{equation}
Reject an edit, comment for another pass, or undo a decision.
These equations describe the workflow; editorial judgment remains with the author.
