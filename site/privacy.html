Who is responsible and what this covers

Chen Cui is the operator and data controller for the Papilot website at papilot.ai. For privacy questions or requests, email i@cuichen.cc.

This notice covers this public website, including this page. It does not describe processing inside the Papilot desktop or command-line application, your papers, your chosen coding agents, or third-party websites.

Hosting and security

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure receives network and request information needed to deliver the site. GitHub states that Pages visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, including when they are not signed into GitHub. This hosting processing happens even if you turn off website statistics.

Providing and protecting this website serves our legitimate interests in availability and preventing abuse, where that legal basis applies. GitHub describes its own processing, retention and international transfers in its privacy statement.

Your choice and browser preferences

The homepage uses cookieless Umami statistics by default. You can turn statistics off or back on for this browser using the settings at the top of this privacy page. Turning statistics off leaves the website and downloads available. This privacy page loads only the local settings script and never loads the analytics service.

Your previously saved refusal is respected unless you change it here. We also disable analytics when the browser signals Do Not Track (DNT) or Global Privacy Control (GPC), or when browser preference storage is unavailable. Turning statistics off stops subsequent collection in open tabs of this site; it does not automatically delete existing records.

We use browser localStorage to remember whether statistics are disabled, with no automatic expiry. Language and appearance preferences also use localStorage and remain until changed or cleared in your browser. These preferences support your requested settings and are separate from analytics retention. Clearing browser storage removes your saved refusal and restores the default, unless a browser privacy signal disables statistics. If your browser cannot save a change, it may not survive a reload or a newly opened tab.

What website statistics measure

When statistics are enabled, we use Umami Cloud to understand how this website is used and improve its content and usability. Our intended legal basis is our legitimate interest in those improvements, where applicable data-protection and ePrivacy rules permit it. Statistics include the localized page and referring website’s origin, plus these fixed categories of activity:

  • Section navigation, external destination categories, and download or language menu openings.
  • Installer link clicks, including platform, architecture, package format and button location; successful or failed installation-command copies, without command contents or error messages.
  • Language and light/dark appearance changes, and real actions in the interactive demonstration: accept, reject, reopen and show/hide source.
  • Section visibility, scroll milestones at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%, and visible-page time at 15, 30, 60 and 120 seconds. These are deduplicated milestones, not a record of every scroll or proof of attention.

We filter outgoing analytics to fixed fields. Query strings, URL fragments, campaign and advertising identifiers, source-page paths, screen dimensions, input contents, clipboard contents and selected text are excluded. We do not add account identifiers or enable session replay or heatmaps. Activity while statistics are disabled is not replayed later, and automatic demo animations are not recorded as user actions. A download click does not establish a completed download or installation.

Umami processes IP addresses and browser User-Agent information to derive visitor/session identifiers and approximate location. Its standard analytics records do not store an IP-address field, but infrastructure or security logs may contain IP addresses. This processing is not a guarantee of complete anonymity. See Umami’s metric definitions and privacy policy.

Service providers, location and retention

Our statistics use Umami Cloud, provided by Umami Software, Inc., with the EU analytics-storage region and Hobby plan. The current Hobby plan retains production analytics for six months. The EU region applies to analytics storage; CDN delivery, security, support and operational logging may involve processing elsewhere. See the Umami Cloud FAQ.

Operational logs and backups have separate retention and deletion terms; the six-month analytics period does not specify their lifetime. Umami’s Data Processing Agreement describes these terms and international-transfer safeguards, including contractual clauses where applicable. Its subprocessor list identifies supporting providers. Website statistics are available to the operator and service providers as needed for the purposes described here.

Your rights and contacting us

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to relevant processing. Contact i@cuichen.cc. We use information you provide to handle your request and may need information reasonably necessary to verify and locate the relevant data. Because our analytics do not include your name or email, those details alone may not identify particular records.

You may also complain to the competent data protection authority, including in your place of habitual residence or work where applicable. The European Data Protection Board maintains a list of European supervisory authorities. We do not use these website statistics to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

External links and changes

Links to downloads, GitHub, npm, documentation and other sites take you to services with their own privacy practices. This notice does not control their processing. We will update this page when the website’s practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision. Changes to analytics purposes or recipients will be reflected in this notice and must comply with the applicable legal requirements.

简体中文

运营者与适用范围

Chen Cui 是 Papilot 官网 papilot.ai 的运营者及数据控制者。如有隐私问题或相关请求，请联系 i@cuichen.cc。

本声明适用于本公开网站，包括本页；不涉及 Papilot 桌面版或命令行应用内部、你的论文、你选择的编程智能体或第三方网站的数据处理。

网站托管与安全

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。托管基础设施会接收提供网页所需的网络和请求信息。GitHub 说明，Pages 访客的 IP 地址会出于安全目的被记录和保存，无论访客是否登录 GitHub。关闭网站统计不会停止这类托管处理。

在适用的情况下，提供和保护网站基于保持网站可用及防止滥用的正当利益。GitHub 自身的数据处理、保留及国际传输情况见其隐私声明。

你的选择与浏览器偏好

主页默认开启不使用 Cookie 的 Umami 统计。你可以通过本隐私页顶部的设置，为当前浏览器关闭或重新开启统计。关闭统计不会影响使用网站和下载。本隐私页只加载本地设置脚本，不加载统计服务。

你此前保存的拒绝选择会继续生效，直到你在此更改。当浏览器发出 Do Not Track（DNT）或 Global Privacy Control（GPC）信号，或浏览器偏好存储不可用时，我们也会关闭统计。关闭统计会停止本网站已打开标签页的后续采集，但不会自动删除已有记录。

我们通过浏览器 localStorage 保存是否关闭统计的选择，不设自动到期时间。语言和外观偏好也使用 localStorage，直到你更改或清除浏览器中的存储。这些偏好用于保留你要求的设置，与统计数据保留期不同。清除浏览器存储会移除已保存的拒绝选择，恢复默认设置；浏览器隐私信号仍可使统计保持关闭。如果浏览器无法保存更改，刷新或新开标签页后该更改可能不再生效。

网站统计记录什么

统计开启时，我们使用 Umami Cloud 了解网站使用情况，改进内容与可用性。拟采用的处理依据是实现这些改进的正当利益，但须符合适用的数据保护及 ePrivacy 规则。统计包含页面语言版本、来源网站的域名及协议，以及以下固定类别的行为：

  • 章节导航、外部链接的目标类别、下载菜单和语言菜单的打开。
  • 安装包链接点击及其平台、架构、格式和按钮位置；安装命令复制成功或失败，不包含命令内容或错误信息。
  • 语言及浅色／深色外观切换，以及交互演示中的真实操作：接受、拒绝、重新打开和显示／隐藏源码。
  • 章节曝光、25%、50%、75%、100% 的滚动里程碑，以及页面可见时累计 15、30、60、120 秒的停留里程碑。这些记录会去重，不是每次滚动的明细，也不代表实际注意力。

发出的统计仅包含固定字段，排除查询参数、网址片段、活动及广告标识、来源页面路径、屏幕尺寸、输入内容、剪贴板内容和选中文字。我们不添加账号标识，不启用会话回放或热图。统计关闭期间的行为不会在之后补报，演示中的自动动画不会作为用户操作上报。点击下载不代表下载完成或已安装。

Umami 会处理 IP 地址和浏览器 User-Agent 信息，用于推导访客／会话标识及大致位置。其标准统计记录不保存 IP 地址字段，但基础设施或安全日志可能包含 IP 地址，因此不能视为完全匿名。详见 Umami 的指标说明和隐私政策。

服务商、地区与保留期限

我们使用 Umami Software, Inc. 提供的 Umami Cloud，选择 EU 统计存储地区和 Hobby 方案。当前 Hobby 方案的生产统计数据保留期为六个月。EU 地区指统计存储；CDN 分发、安全、支持及运维日志等仍可能涉及其他地区的处理。详见 Umami Cloud 常见问题。

运维日志和备份适用独立的保留与删除条款，不能以六个月统计保留期推定它们的保存时间。Umami 的数据处理协议说明相关条款及国际传输保障，包括适用时的合同条款；子处理者名单列出支持服务商。运营者及服务商可为本声明所述目的按需访问网站统计。

依据适用法律，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理和数据可携带权，可反对相关处理。请联系 i@cuichen.cc。我们会使用你提供的信息处理请求，并可能需要合理必要的信息以核验请求和定位数据。由于统计不包含你的姓名或邮箱，仅凭这些信息可能无法确定具体记录。
