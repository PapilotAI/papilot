---
name: Check math
description: Find errors in notation, equations and proofs
hint: Which part? e.g. "Section 3 and Appendix A". Leave empty for the whole paper
order: 4
---
Check the mathematics for errors and inconsistencies.

- Every symbol is defined before it is used and means one thing throughout. Indices, signs, dimensions and quantifiers agree. Theorem statements match their proofs and the places that cite them. References to equations, theorems and figures point to the right ones.
- Fix clear slips directly: a typo in a formula, an inconsistent symbol, a wrong reference.
- Do not change what a result says. If you suspect a substantive error, leave the text and explain the problem in your summary, with its location and your reasoning.
- Follow the paper's own macros and environments.
