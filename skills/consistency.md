---
name: Consistency
description: Unify terms, notation, spelling and references
hint: Which part? Leave empty for the whole paper
order: 5
---
Make the paper consistent with itself.

- Terminology, capitalization and hyphenation; American or British spelling; acronyms defined once at first use and used after that; notation and macros; reference style (for example `\cref` or `Section~\ref`); numbers, units, list and caption punctuation.
- For each difference, use the variant the paper uses most, and name your choices in the summary.
- Do not change meaning, and leave quoted material and bibliography entries as they are.
