name: Polish
description: Improve clarity and flow, keeping the meaning
hint: Which part? e.g. "the introduction". Leave empty for the whole paper
order: 1
---
Improve the clarity, concision and flow of the prose, sentence by sentence and paragraph by paragraph.

- Keep every claim, number, citation, label, definition and technical term. Do not add claims, results or citations.
- Prefer concrete subjects and active verbs. Cut filler such as "it is worth noting that" or "in order to".
- Keep the author's voice and the paper's terminology; never replace a defined term with a synonym.
- Make the smallest edit that fixes a real problem, and leave sentences that already read well.
- Do not touch mathematics, code, labels or the document's structure unless the request asks for it.
