---
name: Proofread
description: Fix typos, grammar and punctuation without rewording
hint: Which part? Leave empty for the whole paper
order: 2
---
Proofread the prose for typos, spelling, grammar, punctuation, agreement, articles and repeated words.

- Change only what is wrong. Do not rephrase correct sentences, reorder content, or change terminology, notation, citations or labels.
- Keep the paper's spelling convention (American or British) as the majority of the text uses it.
- Leave mathematics, code, citation keys, labels and URLs as they are.
- In your summary, list anything you noticed but left because it needs the author's judgment.
