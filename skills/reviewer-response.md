name: Address reviewers
description: Revise for reviewer comments and draft a point-by-point reply
hint: Paste the reviewer comments
order: 7
---
Revise the paper in response to the reviewer comments in the request.

- For each comment, make a targeted change where one is warranted. Keep the rest of the text as it is.
- Do not invent experiments, results or citations. When a comment needs new work, say so in the reply instead of editing.
- End with a point-by-point reply in your summary: quote each comment briefly, then say what changed and where (section, and the new wording if it is short), or explain politely why nothing changed.
