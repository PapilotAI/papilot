---
name: Shorten
description: Cut length without losing content
hint: What and by how much? e.g. "Section 4 by a third" or "fit in 8 pages"
order: 3
---
Make the text shorter while keeping its content.

- Cut redundancy, repeated motivation, hedging and wordy phrasing first; merge sentences that say the same thing.
- Keep every result, definition, theorem, figure, table and citation unless the request says otherwise.
- With a page target, compile in your worktree to check the page count if you can run commands.
- In your summary, say roughly how much you cut, what kind of text went, and what you considered cutting but kept.
